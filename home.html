Welcome to the Unofficial Peopoly Magneto X Community Wiki

The purpose of this wiki is to organize the knowledge that the community has on the Peopoly Magneto X 3D printer. The wiki is currently under construction, so it's a bit rough. Please be patient as we get all of the information onto it.

Navigating the Wiki

When navigating the wiki, you can use the sidebar to find the main categories and articles therein. You can also search by tag if you're looking for something specific.

Contribution Guidelines

Everyone is encouraged to contribute to the wiki. Before creating a new page, check the left side bar to see if it can't be added to an existing page or category first. Because we are currently in the initial build out, we aren't worrying too much about polish or making sure everything is organized perfectly. The important part is to get the knowledge up on the wiki and then we will organize and polish it together as a community.

Links to where to buy specific products are encouraged with the following requirements:

  • Have the name and maker of the product on the wiki so that if the link dies, people can still search for the product elsewhere.
  • Links to the products must have affiliate codes stripped. If the link is to a product or model that you profit monetarily from, please tag cassie_mmk in the “Magneto X Community Wiki” thread in the #magneto-resources channel on Peopoly Discord. These products will be cleared on a case-by-case basis as we recognize that there's a lot of specialty parts that have few sources available.

The most important thing when contributing, aside from the information itself, is to make sure that said information is tagged correctly. This greatly assists with searchability. If you're not sure if a name for a tag is good enough, it's something that can be polished later. Finally, there are three revisions of the Magneto X, if your article is for a specific version, please tag it with the correct version. At the moment those tags are:

  • v1.0 - These are the preorder and initial shipping units
  • v1.5 - These units shipped most of this past summer.
  • v2.0 - These units have been shipping since late fall.

Where to Find the Community

The community can be found on the official Peopoly Discord. Make sure to tag yourself with the Magneto role after joining the server in order to see the Magneto X specific channels.
